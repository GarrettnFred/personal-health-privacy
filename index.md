# Privacy Policy — Personal Health & Performance Hub

**Last updated: November 25, 2026**

## 1. Overview

Personal Health & Performance Hub is a privately operated application designed to synchronize personal health and fitness information from WHOOP into a private Notion workspace.

The application is intended for personal use and is not offered as a public service.

## 2. Information Accessed

With the account holder's authorization, the application may access the following WHOOP information:

- Recovery scores, heart rate variability, and resting heart rate
- Sleep duration, sleep performance, and related measurements
- Workouts, activity types, workout duration, and strain
- Physiological cycle information and daily strain
- Profile and body measurements where necessary

Only information permitted through WHOOP's authorized API is accessed.

## 3. How Information Is Used

The information is used exclusively for personal health tracking, fitness analysis, progress monitoring, and synchronization with an authorized private Notion workspace.

The application does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

## 4. Data Storage and Sharing

Health and fitness measurements may be stored in the account holder's private Notion workspace.

The integration may use private automation infrastructure and securely managed authentication credentials to operate.

Information is transmitted to the services necessary to operate the integration, including WHOOP, Notion, and the selected automation provider.

No health information is intentionally published to the public privacy-policy website.

## 5. Data Security

The integration is designed to use authorized API connections and restrict access to the account holder.

Authentication credentials must be kept private and must not be included in publicly accessible source code.

## 6. Access and Revocation

The account holder can revoke the application's access through WHOOP's account authorization settings.

Synchronized records stored in Notion may be managed or deleted separately.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality, information processing, or third-party services change.

## 8. Contact

For questions regarding this application or its privacy practices, please contact the application administrator using the contact information provided in the WHOOP developer application registration.
